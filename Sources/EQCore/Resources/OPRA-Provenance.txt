This adaptation joins the vendor/product names to their EQ profiles and converts
peak_dip, low_shelf and high_shelf to the app's Q-based, center-frequency filters.
Frequencies, gains, Q, preamp and ordered bands are retained exactly. Creator,
details and original source links are preserved. No curves are blended and no
unsupported filters are approximated or dropped from an included profile.

12,588 complete profiles are included. Six complete profiles are excluded:
five contain slope-only low-pass filters without explicit Q, and one contains
an 8.5 Hz band below the app's 10 Hz minimum. See OPRA-Excluded.json for IDs.

Regenerate from the pinned data with scripts/update-headphone-catalog.py.
The app performs no network request to browse or apply these bundled profiles.
Selected presets retain creator, source, license and conversion attribution.
